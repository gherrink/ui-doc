## Template usage notes

**Lead with symptoms:** Users search by what they see, not by the underlying cause.

**Include error messages:** Quote exact error messages when applicable.

**Order solutions:** Put the most likely solution first.

**Test all solutions:** Every solution must actually work.

**Multiple problems per document:** Group related problems together.

**Keep focused:** Don't explain background concepts; link to conceptual guides instead.
